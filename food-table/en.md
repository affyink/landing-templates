## Good food starts with good ingredients

{{companyName}} makes food and kitchen products for people who like to cook, host and share. We pay attention to where ingredients come from, how they are prepared and how they taste on an ordinary Tuesday, not only at a special dinner.

## Who we would like to cook with

- Home cooks and recipe creators who share what they actually make.
- Reviewers of snacks, drinks, pantry items and kitchen tools.
- Hosts, families and meal-prep channels who show products in daily use.

## Ideas that work well

- A recipe where our product is a real part of the dish, with the steps shown.
- A taste test with honest notes, including who it might not suit.
- A simple weekday meal made with a few of our products.

## A few ground rules

Please state allergens and preparation details correctly and check them on the product page. Do not make health claims about food. Let people know that your link is a partnership link; a short sentence near the link is enough.

## How it goes

1. Apply with the button above and tell us what you cook.
2. After approval, choose what you would like to try.
3. Share your cooking with your personal link. We are happy to answer questions about ingredients any time through [{{websiteHost}}]({{websiteUrl}}).
